LANCET I

Dense gas evolution in G316.8

Capture the evolution of dense gas in the 14-parsec filament G316.8. The first study connects dense-fragment statistics, column-density distributions, and cloud structure. Published in A&A 708, A251 (2026). Read Paper I on SciX ↗

LANCET II / IN REVISION

Hierarchical mass assembly

Hierarchical mass assembly from filamentary clouds to dense cores in G316.8. The current manuscript explores how structures at different scales participate in mass assembly.
